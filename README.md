# Waldorf Math Pathway

A first repository for a Waldorf-inspired Grade 5 through Grade 9 online math practice site.

The goal is to create a child-friendly website similar in spirit to a Waldorf
pathway: gentle lessons, practical arithmetic, story problems, and exercises
children can complete online. Grade 5 now has a first block for decimal
fractions, metric measure, fraction review, symmetry, ancient measures, and
freehand geometry. Grade 6 focuses on business math, percentages, budgeting,
simple interest, and geometry. Grade 7 continues with gentle arithmetic
practice, story problems, and rhythmic number work. Grade 8 adds number bases,
scientific notation, Pythagorean theorem, percent growth, dimensional analysis,
proportions, exponent laws, and mensuration. Grade 9 extends the path into
Algebra I readiness with equations, systems, negative exponents, radicals,
linear functions, quadratics, coordinate geometry, and growth models.

## Current Learning Experience

- Static website that opens from `index.html`
- Original Grade 5 through Grade 9 sample math exercises
- Gentle answer checking
- Student-first lesson flow: warm up, discover, practise, check, and reflect
- Grade filtering so learners can stay inside the right developmental block
- Try-first activities with examples and methods hidden behind optional support
- Three levels of feedback after incorrect attempts: a noticing prompt, a method prompt, then guided-answer access
- Guided worksheet-style activities with Portuguese examples, step checks, and answer keys
- Extra practice sets and a student reflection prompt after successful work
- Three curated bilingual practice variations for each of the 20 foundational Grade 5 and early Grade 6 lessons that previously had no practice bank
- Daily Fact Rhythm with 60 balanced original arithmetic facts, learning and fluency paths, optional timing, personal records, and a six-fact recovery round
- Waldorf-inspired visual direction
- Supabase Auth and progress saving through the same guardian/learner tables as Waldorf English Pathway

## Curriculum Note

The initial exercise examples are original. Jamie York-style ideas or other
published curriculum materials should be adapted only from notes you own, with
permission, or by creating original exercises inspired by the same learning
goals rather than copying protected workbook text.

The attached middle-school source is organized through eighth grade and notes
that fuller algebra belongs mainly in ninth grade. The Grade 9 lessons here are
therefore original bridge lessons that extend the Grade 8 goals into common
Algebra I preparation rather than copied book material.

## Suggested Next Steps

1. Deepen the Grade 5 decimal fractions and freehand geometry block with drawing prompts.
2. Deepen the Grade 6 business math block with more interactive portfolio tasks.
3. Add three to five curated practice variations to every lesson; do not generate them by merely changing numbers unless the mathematical conditions are checked.
4. Add visual manipulatives for fractions, ratios, signed numbers, geometry, and early algebra.
5. Record hints used and independent mastery in the learner portfolio once the shared database schema is ready.
6. Add a fuller math portfolio view once the English portfolio is ready to show subject-specific filters.
