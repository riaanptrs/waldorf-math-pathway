# Codex Project Notes

## Project

Waldorf Math Pathway is a static Waldorf-inspired Grade 5 through Grade 9 math practice site with gentle exercises, answer checking, quick tutor flows, and Supabase progress-saving plans.

## First Steps

1. Read `README.md` and `docs/CURRENT_STATUS.md`.
2. Keep Supabase secrets, learner data, and account credentials out of Git.
3. Keep exercises original or properly licensed; do not copy protected workbook text.
4. Preserve direct static-site usability from `index.html`.

## Content Rules

- Keep the tone child-friendly and practical.
- Use Portuguese examples where they support learning.
- Treat Grade 9 as original Algebra I bridge work rather than copied source material.
