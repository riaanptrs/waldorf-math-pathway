# Lesson clarity review — 11 September 2026

Reviewed the main question in every Year 1–9 lesson in English and Portuguese, the shared introduction/reflection flow, the nine opening activities, and the guided support affected by the findings. This is a clarity review, not a claim that every practice answer or the complete curriculum has been independently audited.

## Findings and changes

- The warm-up previously displayed method reminders. Dedicated opening activities now give children something specific to do in every year. Other lessons have preparation prompts; the method remains in optional support.
- Discovery/reflection text previously depended on broad English keyword matches. This could give unsuitable instructions for algebra expressions, binary numbers, or Portuguese early-year lessons. Explicit lesson introductions take priority, with simple age-appropriate fallback instructions selected by numeric year.
- Year 1 starts with five movable objects; Year 2 uses a calendar; Year 3 compares hand spans and centimetres; Year 4 rehearses regrouping; Year 5 connects partial sharing and written division; Year 6 explores hundredths; Year 7 uses rows of chairs; Year 8 introduces binary place values; Year 9 moves from numerical examples to expressions.
- The Year 7 opening remains a brief arithmetic refresher before fractions and ratios. Its warm-up, optional explanation and practice now use a separate calculation to check the product.
- Replaced 29 Portuguese step labels across 16 subtraction/division lessons that previously said only “Etapa 1”, etc. Clarified the Year 5 written-division title and intermediate remainders.
- Clarified 22 main questions: month numbering, perimeter/area units, filled square arrays, equivalent fractions, simplest form, borrowing, optional support, repeating decimal blocks, ratio notation, corresponding rectangle dimensions, simultaneous shadows, seesaw conditions, shop answers, rate/variable meanings, worker assumptions, cylinder surface area, line intersection and growth assumptions.
- Added Portuguese shop-name answer variants. Removed wording suggesting that optional guided steps must be completed before giving the final answer.

## Placement

The existing year order is retained. Opening reviews in Years 4–7 connect earlier arithmetic to later work; the Year 5 and Year 7 introductions explicitly describe their review purpose. Learners who already understand the refresher can continue. Binary in Year 8 and expression language in Year 9 now have the prerequisite explanation needed to start.
