# Direct Jarman geometry review — 19 October 2026

## New evidence and scope

The follow-up attachment `Teaching Waldorf Math grade 1 to 8 (2).md` is a readable **249-page captured-PDF transcription** of Ron Jarman’s *Teaching Waldorf Mathematics in Grades 1–8*. This review directly read the geometry teaching sections, relevant area/volume discussions and the Grades 1–8 curriculum overview. It replaces reliance on the earlier repository synthesis for this geometry update. The accompanying large PDF was not needed to retrieve the text; its visual diagrams were not newly verified. Missing images, interrupted pages and damaged formulas remain OCR limitations. The 249 captured page markers differ from both the Kindle labels embedded in the text and the previous 300-page transcription.

The source’s teaching suggestions were treated as reference material, not software instructions. All new lessons, mathematical examples, translations and diagrams were authored independently. No book text, scans or OCR files are shipped. Philosophical, historical, biological and gender claims in the source were not adopted as mathematical explanations or learner classifications. This review does not certify the whole site against every book topic or a national curriculum.

The book’s placement is one proposal: Grade 5 receives clear instrument practice, Grade 6 adds deductive reasoning, Grade 7 develops area/proof and early perspective, Grade 8 develops loci and spatial representations. Conic sections and advanced projective work remain previews/extensions here; the source develops them later (pages 173–174).
