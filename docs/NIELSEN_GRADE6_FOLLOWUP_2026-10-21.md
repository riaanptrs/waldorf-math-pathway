## Source discrepancies and limits

- The general recommendation table mentions ten math/geometry weeks for Classes
  6–8, but this particular Grade 6 schedule specifies three four-week blocks.
  The map preserves the volume's actual block schedule instead of silently
  forcing it into ten weeks.
- The guidance specifies 12/8/4 practice weeks. Its sheet labels are 1–12,
  13–21 (nine sheets) and 22–25; some middle headers also say twelve sheets.
  The original online plan follows the stated durations, groups middle review
  into eight weeks and explicitly flags the inconsistency. It does not claim
  exact sheet-to-week equivalence. Further review can extend any phase.
- Images and companion videos were not supplied. New models are mathematically
  defined original adaptations, not reconstructions of missing source artwork.
- Named-platform fees and travel prices in the source are dated examples, not
  current quotations. New figures are fictional and labelled. A credit-payoff
  example states its monthly rate, order of interest/payment and cent rounding;
  it is distinct from a simple-interest calculation.
- Adjacent angles need not sum to 180° unless they form a straight angle;
  corresponding-angle equality needs parallel lines. Geometry descriptions
  keep these conditions explicit through the reused lessons.
- Historical claims about a single origin/path of money or dated banking rules
  are not treated as verified universal facts by this math-source review.
